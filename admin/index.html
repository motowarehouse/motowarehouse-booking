Admin User Manual

Everything you need to manage the Motowarehouse Service Booking Portal.

Bookings
How do I accept or cancel a booking?

Go to All Bookings or Pending. Each booking card has a green ✓ button (accept) and a red ✕ button (cancel) on the right. Click the card to expand it and see full details before deciding. When you accept, the customer receives a confirmation email automatically.

How do I reschedule a booking?

Expand the booking card by clicking it, then use the Reschedule button inside. Pick a new date and time — the customer will receive a reschedule notification email.

What does "Needs Call" mean?

Some bookings come in without a confirmed time — usually walk-ins or enquiries. These appear in the Needs Call queue (orange badge in the sidebar). Call the customer to confirm a date and time, then manually accept or add them as a block.

Block Time
How do I block a time slot?

Go to Block Time in the sidebar. Select a date — the From and To dropdowns will automatically show only valid time slots for that day based on your opening hours. Fill in a reason (optional) and click Block This Time. Blocked slots will not be available for online bookings.

How do I remove a block?

Scroll down to Active Blocks on the same page. Click the ✕ Remove button on any block to delete it — those time slots become available again immediately.

Opening Hours
How do I change opening hours?

Go to Opening Hours. Toggle each day on or off, and set the opening and closing times. Changes take effect immediately for the customer booking form. Wednesday is morning-only by default.

Vehicles
How do I search for a vehicle?

Go to Vehicles and type the registration plate number into the search box. The full vehicle record will appear, including model, frame number, engine number, and complete service history.

How do I import the weekly vehicle list?

Every Monday, export the latest MOTO MOVEMENT Excel file (.xlsm) from your system. In the Vehicles tab, drag and drop the file onto the import zone — or click it to browse. The portal reads all year sheets automatically and updates the vehicle database. The 7-day countdown badge shows when the next import is due.

Partners
How do I create a partner account?

Go to Partners and fill in the workshop name, username, and password. Click Create Account. Share the username and password with the workshop — they log in at the partner portal URL. You can also delete partner accounts from the same page.

How do I reset a partner's password?

If a partner forgets their password, go to Partners, find the account, delete it, and create a new one with the same workshop name but a new password.

Warranty Inbox
How do I review warranty claims?

Go to Warranty in the sidebar — the orange badge shows how many open claims are waiting. Each claim shows the registration plate, workshop, priority level, fault description, and any photos. Click a photo thumbnail to view it full screen.

How do I approve or reject a warranty claim?

Expand the claim, add optional internal notes in the text box, then click Approve or Reject. The claim moves to the appropriate filter tab (Approved / Rejected).

Will I be notified of new warranty claims?

Yes — an email is sent to info@motowarehouse.com.cy as soon as a partner submits a claim, including the registration plate, workshop name, priority level, and fault description.
