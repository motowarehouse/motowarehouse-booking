Admin User Manual
Complete guide to managing the Motowarehouse Service Booking Portal.
Bookings
How do I accept or cancel a booking?
  • 1Go to All Bookings or Pending in the sidebar.
  • 2Click a booking card to expand it and see full details.
  • 3Use the green ✓ button to accept, or red ✕ to cancel. The customer receives an email automatically.
How do I reschedule a booking?
  • 1Click the booking card to expand it.
  • 2Click the Reschedule button and pick a new date and time.
  • 3The customer receives a reschedule notification email automatically.
What does "Needs Call" mean?

Bookings that came in without a confirmed time slot appear here (orange badge in the sidebar). Call the customer to confirm a date and time, then accept the booking or add it as a manual block.

Block Time
How do I block a time slot?
  • 1Go to Block Time in the sidebar.
  • 2Select a date — the From and To dropdowns will show only valid slots for that day.
  • 3Fill in a reason (optional) and click Block This Time.
Blocked slots are immediately unavailable for online bookings.
How do I remove a block?

Scroll down to Active Blocks on the same page. Click the ✕ Remove button on any block — those time slots become available again immediately.

Opening Hours
How do I change opening hours?
  • 1Go to Opening Hours in the sidebar.
  • 2Toggle each day on or off and set the opening and closing times.
  • 3Click Save Hours. Changes take effect immediately on the customer booking form.
Vehicles
How do I search for a vehicle?

Go to Vehicles and type the registration plate number. The full vehicle record appears — model, frame number, engine number, and complete service history with all partner entries.

How do I import the weekly vehicle list?
  • 1Every Monday, export the updated MOTO MOVEMENT Excel file (.xlsm).
  • 2In the Vehicles tab, drag and drop the file onto the import zone — or click it to browse.
  • 3The portal reads all year sheets automatically and updates the vehicle database.
The 7-day countdown badge in the Vehicles tab shows when the next import is due.
Partners
How do I create a partner account?
  • 1Go to Partners in the sidebar.
  • 2Fill in the workshop name, username, and a password.
  • 3Click Create Account and share the credentials with the workshop.
How do I reset a partner's password?

Go to Partners, find the account and delete it, then create a new one with the same workshop name and a new password.

Warranty Inbox
How do I review warranty claims?

Go to Warranty in the sidebar. The orange badge shows pending claims. Each card shows the plate, workshop, priority, fault description, and any photos. Click any photo to view it full screen.

How do I approve or reject a claim?
  • 1Open the claim card and read the fault description and photos.
  • 2Add internal notes if needed (only visible to you).
  • 3Click Approve or Reject. The claim moves to the appropriate tab.
Will I be notified of new claims?

Yes — an email is sent to motowarehouse.bookings@gmail.com immediately when a partner submits a claim, with the plate, workshop, priority, and fault description.

Mechanic Notes
How do I add notes for the mechanic?
  • 1Open an Accepted booking by clicking on it.
  • 2Find the Mechanic Notes text box at the bottom of the card.
  • 3Type any internal instructions and click Save Notes. Notes are internal — the customer never sees them.
Dashboard
What does the Dashboard show?

Go to Dashboard in the sidebar. You'll see: today's accepted bookings, pending count, all-time totals (completed, no-shows, cancelled), weekly booking trend over the last 4 weeks, service type breakdown for the last 30 days, and a day-of-week chart showing your busiest days.

Print Day Schedule
How do I print today's schedule?
  • 1Go to Today's Schedule in the sidebar.
  • 2Click 🖨️ Print Schedule at the top right.
  • 3A clean print-ready page opens in a new tab with a table of all appointments — time, customer, vehicle, service type, mechanic assignment, and notes. Click Print or use Ctrl+P.
