Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective immediately

This Cookie Policy explains how the Motowarehouse service booking portal at bookings.motowarehouse.com.cy uses cookies and similar technologies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work properly and to provide information to the site operator.

Cookies We Use

This portal uses only strictly necessary and functional cookies. We do not use advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies.

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
connect.sid Strictly necessary Session management — maintains your login state if you are an authorised workshop partner or admin. Not set for regular customers booking an appointment. Session (deleted when browser closes)
hCaptcha cookies Functional / Security Set by hCaptcha (Intuition Machines, Inc.) for bot detection and security verification on the booking form. These cookies do not track you for advertising purposes. Session / short-term

Cookies We Do NOT Use

  • Analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics)
  • Advertising or retargeting cookies
  • Social media tracking cookies
  • Any third-party profiling cookies

Google Fonts

This portal loads fonts from Google Fonts (Google LLC). When your browser requests a font file from Google's servers, your IP address is transmitted to Google. This is a standard technical process. Google may use this data in accordance with their own privacy policy. We use Google Fonts solely to display the portal interface correctly.

Your Choices

Since this portal only uses strictly necessary and security-related cookies, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the portal. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

More Information

For more information about how we handle your personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
