Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective immediately

This Cookie Policy explains how the Motowarehouse service booking portal at bookings.motowarehouse.com.cy uses cookies and similar technologies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are used to make websites function correctly and to provide information to the site operator.

Cookies We Use

This portal uses only strictly necessary and functional/security cookies. We do not use advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies of any kind.

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
connect.sid Strictly necessary Session management — maintains login state for authorised workshop partners and admins only. Not set for regular customers submitting a booking. 7 days (persistent)
hCaptcha cookies Functional / Security Set by hCaptcha (Intuition Machines, Inc.) for bot detection and security verification on the booking form. Not used for advertising. Up to 1 year

Cookies We Do Not Use

  • Analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics, Hotjar)
  • Advertising or retargeting cookies
  • Social media tracking cookies
  • Third-party profiling cookies of any kind

Web Fonts

This portal uses the Inter typeface, which is served directly from our own server. No font requests are made to Google Fonts or any third-party font service. Your IP address is not transmitted to any external server for the purpose of loading fonts.

Managing Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling the connect.sid session cookie will prevent workshop partners and admins from staying logged in. For regular customers submitting a booking, no session cookie is set — only the hCaptcha security cookies apply.

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
