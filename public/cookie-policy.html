Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective immediately

This Cookie Policy explains how the Motowarehouse service booking portal at bookings.motowarehouse.com.cy uses cookies and similar technologies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are used to make websites function correctly and to provide information to the site operator.

Cookies We Use

This portal uses only strictly necessary and functional/security cookies. We do not use advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies of any kind.

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
connect.sid Strictly necessary Session management — maintains login state for authorised workshop partners and admins only. Not set for regular customers submitting a booking. Session (deleted on browser close)
hCaptcha cookies Functional / Security Set by hCaptcha (Intuition Machines, Inc.) for bot detection and security verification on the booking form. Not used for advertising. Session / short-term

Cookies We Do Not Use

  • Analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics, Hotjar)
  • Advertising or retargeting cookies
  • Social media tracking cookies
  • Third-party profiling cookies of any kind

Google Fonts

This portal loads fonts from Google Fonts (Google LLC). When your browser requests a font file, your IP address is transmitted to Google's servers as a standard technical process. Google may process this data in accordance with their own privacy policy. We use Google Fonts solely to display the portal interface correctly.

Managing Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling session cookies may affect portal functionality for workshop partners. For regular customers, no persistent cookies are set during the booking process.

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
